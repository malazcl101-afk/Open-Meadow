OPEN MEADOW by HarborNestDigital — quick start

1. Unzip this package. Keep all files together.
2. On a computer, open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser. For phone/tablet use, host the same files on HTTPS (for example, a private site you control); then open that URL in your browser. See README.html for hosting and home-screen steps.
3. Start with Students, then add Subjects. Plan lessons, enter scores on those lessons, and log attendance. Gradebook and reports calculate from your records. Family (including routines and check-ins), School year, Assessments, Assignments, Projects, Progress, Enrichment, and Records organize the remaining workflows. Portfolio attachments are limited to 2 MB per image/PDF and are included in backups.
4. Open Customize to set your school name/year, grade weights, colors, font, spacing, card style, contrast, and currency display.
5. In Customize, choose Download JSON backup regularly. To move records to another browser or device, open the planner there and choose Restore backup.

IMPORTANT: Your records live only in this browser profile on this device. There is no account or automatic cross-device sync. Clearing browser/site data or using a different profile can remove local records. Keep downloaded backups somewhere safe. The sample demo is separate from your clean customer planner.

This is a planning and record-keeping tool, not an official transcript or guarantee of local homeschooling compliance. Check your own requirements before using records for reporting.
